# 01 — CITY OVERVIEW

## 1.1 Identity

New York City is the largest city in the United States by population and one of the world's major centers for finance, commerce, media, technology, culture, fashion, education, tourism and international business.

The city is located at the southern end of New York State, where the Hudson River meets the Atlantic Ocean through New York Harbor.

New York City consists of five boroughs:

1. Manhattan
2. Brooklyn
3. Queens
4. The Bronx
5. Staten Island

The five-borough structure has existed since the consolidation of Greater New York in 1898.

NYC Planning identifies approximately:

* **304.8 square miles** of city area
* **520 miles of shoreline**
* **6,300+ miles of streets**
* **31,000+ acres of parkland**
* **59 community districts**
* **5 boroughs**

NYC Planning's 2022 city profile reported a population of **8,804,190** based on the 2020 Census. More recent city planning work using Census estimates places the population at approximately **8.26 million in 2023**. Different population figures therefore correspond to different reference years and methodologies.

# 04 — HISTORY

## 4.1 Indigenous New York

Before European colonization, the region was inhabited by Indigenous peoples, particularly the **Lenape**.

The territory is historically associated with **Lenapehoking**.

Indigenous communities used the area's waterways and paths for:

* Trade
* Transportation
* Hunting
* Fishing
* Agriculture
* Communication

Some historic Indigenous routes later influenced the development of modern roads.

---

## 4.2 European Exploration

### 1524

Giovanni da Verrazzano explored the region while sailing for France.

### 1609

Henry Hudson sailed into the region while working for the Dutch East India Company.

His expedition explored the river that would later become known as the **Hudson River**.

### 1613

Dutch trading activity became established in the region.

### 1624

Dutch settlers established settlements in New Netherland.

### 1625

New Amsterdam became the administrative center of the Dutch colony.

### 1626

Peter Minuit is traditionally associated with the Dutch acquisition of Manhattan.

The Dutch established Fort Amsterdam and expanded the settlement.

NYC historical records document the establishment of New Amsterdam and the development of Manhattan as the administrative and commercial center of New Netherland.

---

# 05 — NEW AMSTERDAM

## 5.1 Dutch Period

New Amsterdam developed as:

* Trading center
* Port
* Administrative center
* Immigration hub
* Commercial settlement

The settlement attracted people from different European countries and religious backgrounds.

Historical records document Dutch, German, English, Scandinavian, French, Jewish and other communities.

The colony's history is also inseparable from slavery and the Atlantic slave trade. Enslaved Africans were brought to New Amsterdam from the early colonial period.

---

# 06 — BRITISH NEW YORK

## 6.1 1664

The English seized New Amsterdam.

The settlement was renamed:

**New York**

The name honored James, Duke of York.

The Dutch briefly retook the city in 1673 and renamed it **New Orange**, but English control was restored in 1674.

---

# 07 — AMERICAN REVOLUTION

New York became strategically important during the American Revolutionary War.

### 1776

British forces occupied New York City.

The city remained under British control for much of the Revolutionary War.

### 1783

British forces evacuated the city.

This became known as:

**Evacuation Day**

---

# 08 — NEW YORK AS CAPITAL

After independence, New York became extremely important to the new United States.

### 1785

New York City became the capital of the State of New York.

### 1789

George Washington was inaugurated as the first President of the United States at Federal Hall.

### 1789–1790

New York City served as the national capital of the United States.

The federal capital was later moved to Philadelphia and eventually Washington, D.C.

# 12 — GREATER NEW YORK — 1898

One of the most important events in the city's history occurred in:

**1898**

The former independent municipalities and surrounding areas were consolidated into Greater New York.

The five-borough structure was created:

* Manhattan
* Brooklyn
* Bronx
* Queens
* Richmond/Staten Island

This transformed New York into one of the world's largest urban municipalities.

In 1900, the newly consolidated city had approximately **3.44 million inhabitants**.

into a single interactive experience.

**CITY → BOROUGH → NEIGHBORHOOD → LOCATION → EVENT → PERSON → SOURCE**

without leaving the application.

This structure makes New York City suitable for an interactive digital archive rather than a conventional informational website.
